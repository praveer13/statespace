**Stage.** S6 · **Medium.** SQL

## Exercise 1 · Call the statement alone

The agent says (unreviewed):

> “Holds are serializable by construction; the database guarantees it.”

**The requirement.** No two team debits commit together when, run one after the other, the second would be refused: the team total never ends below zero, however the writers interleave.

**Statement · SQL**

```
-- db/holds.sql: place a hold of 80 on account 1 (PostgreSQL 18.6)
BEGIN ISOLATION LEVEL REPEATABLE READ;
select a.bal - coalesce((select sum(amt) from holds h where h.acct = 1),0) from acct a where a.id = 1;
insert into holds values (1, 80);  -- only when the select returned at least 80
COMMIT;  -- the service retries the whole hold on SQLSTATE 40001
```

The learner answers "Does the statement justify the claim?" with a confidence before reading on.
