# D-30 · Kani

> Draft: first-learner pass pending.

**Format.** Deferred drawer: always open, and it unlocks nothing.

**What it is.**

Kani is a bounded model checker for Rust. It checks properties of Rust code for every input up to the bounds it is given.

**Why the roadmap defers it.**

The roadmap defers Kani until a concrete proof obligation or implementation demands it.

**What surfaces it.**

Surfaces in Today when your overflow erosion fires, where PostgreSQL refuses a credit that your model commits (run RUN-pg-claims), and your runtime has an `i64` path, where `checked_add` returns None on overflow (run RUN-rust-i64). The card then names the overflowing credit as the obligation, says what a Kani result would earn (MODEL-CHECKED [M], with its bound) and lists the erosion's remedies. Nothing changes unless you open it.

## Runs cited

- RUN-pg-claims (postgres 18.6)
- RUN-rust-i64 (rustc 1.96.0)
