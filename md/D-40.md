**What it is.**

A multi-framework SDK is a set of client libraries generated for several frameworks from one interface.

The roadmap defers multi-framework SDKs until a concrete proof obligation or implementation demands it.

**What surfaces it.**

Surfaces in Today when a claim or a parked goal carries the topic `multi-framework-sdks`. The card then names the obligation and adds nothing beyond the roadmap's deferral.
