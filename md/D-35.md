# D-35 · Cryptographic protocol verification

> Draft: first-learner pass pending.

**Format.** Deferred drawer: always open, and it unlocks nothing.

**What it is.**

Cryptographic protocol verification proves secrecy and authentication properties of a protocol against an active attacker.

**Why the roadmap defers it.**

The roadmap defers full cryptographic protocol verification until a concrete proof obligation or implementation demands it.

**What surfaces it.**

Surfaces in Today when a claim or a parked goal carries the topic `crypto-protocols`. The card then names the obligation and notes that the observer model of the secrecy stage assumes no cryptography.
