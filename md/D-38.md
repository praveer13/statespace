# D-38 · Generalized migrations

> Draft: first-learner pass pending.

**Format.** Deferred drawer: always open, and it unlocks nothing.

**What it is.**

Generalized migrations means showing that schema and data migrations preserve your claims in general, rather than for one change.

**Why the roadmap defers it.**

The roadmap defers generalized migrations until a concrete proof obligation or implementation demands it.

**What surfaces it.**

Surfaces in Today when a claim or a parked goal carries the topic `migrations`, as when your final report writes schema migrations as NOT COVERED [N]. The card then says to state the migration as an ASSUMED [A] or NOT COVERED [N] line until an obligation demands more.
