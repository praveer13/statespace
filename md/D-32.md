# D-32 · Proof certificates (Alethe and Carcara)

> Draft: first-learner pass pending.

**Format.** Deferred drawer: always open, and it unlocks nothing.

**What it is.**

A proof certificate is a proof of `unsat` that a solver emits and an independent checker replays. cvc5 emits Alethe proofs, and Carcara checks them.

**Why the roadmap defers it.**

The roadmap defers proof-certificate infrastructure until a concrete proof obligation or implementation demands it.

**What surfaces it.**

Surfaces in Today when the solver-bug erosion fires: on the inputs of one public issue an older Z3 release answered `unsat` on satisfiable inputs (run RUN-z3-11179). It surfaces again when your final report logs a doubt about a flaw the checkers share. The card then shows a cvc5 Alethe proof that contains a `hole` step (run RUN-cvc5-alethe). A certificate with a gap is partial, so "uncertified" stays on solver-only stamps until a certificate replays.

- RUN-z3-11179 (z3 5.3.1)
