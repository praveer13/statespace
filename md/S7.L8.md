## Revocation over traces, then replicas (16 min)

Revocation can promise two different things, and both are temporal statements, written as in S5. **S-new** says that after revocation no new read succeeds: `□[ReadOK(u, d) ⇒ canread(u, d)]_vars`, a safety property. **S-past** says that revocation removes what was already read: `□[Revoke(u, d) ⇒ d ∉ delivered′[u]]_vars`.

**Cell 1** (INS-interp)

The interpreter replays one trace on a single-copy model, where `Open` checks the current ACL, and evaluates both statements over it. `delivered[u]` is the log of every page returned to u.

Trace asserts: a share, a read, a revocation and a second read. Each statement is judged over the behavior this trace produces.

```text
trace
  Share(contract -> dana)
  Open(dana, contract)
  Revoke(dana, contract)
  Open(dana, contract)

S-new   □[ReadOK(u, d) ⇒ canread(u, d)]_vars
S-past  □[Revoke(u, d) ⇒ d ∉ delivered′[u]]_vars
```

**Your call before the first run:**

- B. No
