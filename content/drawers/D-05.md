## Dominoes, precisely {minutes=3}

To prove P(n) for every natural number n, prove P(0), then prove P(d) → P(d + 1) for an arbitrary d. Together they cover every n. For lists: P([]) and P(xs) → P(x :: xs).

The step assumes P(d), the induction hypothesis, and proves P(d + 1). Assuming P(d + 1) is circular. Lean rejects a proof that applies its own statement to the same argument, because nothing shrinks: the circular variant in lesson S2.L4 fails to show termination {{run:RUN-lean-s2-cells}}.

Cases are not induction. Checking P(0) through P(1000) shows those cases and no others. `decide` can check finitely many cases, which is a bounded claim, not the theorem for every n.

## Induction over steps: invariants {minutes=3}

For a system with Init and Step: if Init ⇒ Inv (the base) and Inv ∧ Step ⇒ Inv′ (the step), then Inv holds in every reachable state, by induction on the number of steps. Example: the guarded transfer keeps both balances at zero or above. Base: the initial balances are at zero or above. Step: `amt ≤ a` gives `a − amt ≥ 0`, and `amt > 0` keeps `b + amt ≥ 0`.

An invariant is not always inductive. A counter starts at 0 and adds 2 at each step. "x ≠ 1" holds in every reachable state (0, 2, 4, and so on), yet it fails the step check: from x = −1, which satisfies x ≠ 1, one step reaches 1. The state −1 is unreachable, so "x ≠ 1" is invariant but not inductive. Strengthen it to "x is even", which is inductive and implies x ≠ 1. Lesson S4.L4 sorts four invariants into this two-by-two, and lesson S4.L6 decides whether a satisfiable step is a counterexample to induction or a reachable bug.

## Generalize first {minutes=1}

When the statement mentions a state that changes at every step, the hypothesis must hold for every starting state. `run_conserves` is proved by list induction `generalizing s` {{run:RUN-lean-ledger}}, and without it the step cannot be proved {{run:RUN-lean-ih-ungeneralised}}. Lesson S2.L7 teaches this.

For Rust readers: a `debug_assert!` at the top of a loop checks the invariant on the states the loop visits. Where it breaks: the inductive step covers every state that satisfies the invariant, reachable or not, which is why "x ≠ 1" fails the step check yet never fires in any run.
