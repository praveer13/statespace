## What the game is, and when it does not load {minutes=2}

The Natural Number Game is a disclosed, uncredited link-out. It runs its own Lean version, so a few tactics behave differently from the Bench, and your progress stays in the game: the course credits nothing done there. Lesson S2.L1 asks for four worlds: Tutorial, Addition, Multiplication and Implication.

Its host has not always answered, and a third-party page that failed to load cannot be detected from here, so this drawer is always linked beside the game and never inferred.

## Three fallbacks {minutes=2}

1. The community's in-browser port, at lean.cau.li/game, also a disclosed, uncredited link-out. It is a large download, and the card shows its size before anything loads {{run:RUN-lean-wasm}}.
2. The game from its repository at a pinned commit, built in the devcontainer (D-15). It brings its own Lean toolchain.
3. Original induction warm-ups in the lab template, on the course's own Lean {{run:RUN-lean-ledger}}: k deposits of 2, doubling, and one fee per step, from the bank in lesson S2.L1 {{run:RUN-lean-s2-cells}}.

## What to carry back {minutes=1}

The base case and the step. The hypothesis the step may use (D-05). And the habit of predicting each goal before you run a tactic (lesson S2.L3).
