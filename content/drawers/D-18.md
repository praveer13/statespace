## Two obligations, and bounded checkers {minutes=3}

An inductive invariant satisfies Init ⇒ Inv and Inv ∧ Next ⇒ Inv′. The second is checked from every state the type invariant allows, reachable or not (Lamport's technique; D-05).

TLC enumerates those states for fixed constants. Apalache encodes the check for an SMT solver but still instantiates the constants and types it is given. Either result earns MODEL-CHECKED [M], with the constants and the words "inductive at N = k", never PROVED alone.

## For every N {minutes=2}

Discharge the step with the parameter as an uninterpreted sort in Z3, or prove it in Lean, and the result is PROVED [P] of the encoding, uncertified, with a premise witness and the open defeater "encoding unverified" for the SMT translation of the spec {{run:RUN-z3-inductive-param}}. TLC's check at one fixed N sits beside it {{run:RUN-tlc-inductive}}.

## Running Apalache, and a gap to spot {minutes=2}

Apalache is optional, on the Bench. Its version is recorded with a bounded smoke check that says nothing more {{run:RUN-apalache-version}}.

A gap to spot: "TLC inductive check at N = 3, stamped PROVED for all N" is a bounded result presented as proof.
