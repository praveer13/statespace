## Two commands, paired {minutes=3}

`run` asks Alloy for an instance that satisfies a predicate within a scope. `check` asks for a counterexample to an assertion within a scope. A check that finds none says nothing beyond its scope: MODEL-CHECKED [M], with the scope, complete within it.

A check over a contradictory model passes vacuously, the same trap as an `unsat` without a premise witness (D-02), so pair every `check` with a satisfiable `run`.

## The small scope hypothesis {minutes=1}

Daniel Jackson's argument is that many bugs have small counterexamples, so bounded analysis raises no false alarms but cannot prove anything. It is a hypothesis about where bugs usually live, not a theorem about every size.

## Subset is not overlap {minutes=1}

`s in t` says s is a subset of t, and on an empty s it holds vacuously. Overlap is `some s & t`.
