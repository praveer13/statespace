Restate CL-3.3 from memory. When a shared view leaks a private field, which part of its strip cracks?

- A. Proposition of the model
  - why: Every successful step still preserves the invariant.
  - mis: dp:L4
- B. Correspondence
  - why: The leak is in the view's own semantics, which is the model.
  - mis: dp:P3
- C. Requirement fit
  - why: The theorem never said who learns what, so confidentiality becomes [N] on CL-3.3.

:::item role=prequestion family=GEN-leaks-to-observer template=t1 kcs=KC-7.declassification

Birch shares contract with dana, then edits its body. dana sees the new body. Is that a leak?

- A. Yes: a tenant observed a change in another tenant.
  - why: That is the blanket claim, and sharing exists to permit this.
  - mis: dp:A15
- B. No: it is the release Birch chose, working as intended.
  - why: The share released contract's title and body to dana.

## Finding the leak, then releasing on purpose {minutes=16 kcs=KC-7.declassification,KC-7.side-channels}

The shared page now also returns `internal_notes`. Check it by pairing: related worlds agree on Acme's data and on the released title and body of shared documents.

:::instrument ins=INS-pairs cell=1 recorded=RUN-z3-leaky-field
The viewer replays the pair that Z3 finds for the self-composition query.

```text
view Open(viewer, id) =
  if no document has this id        then error not_found
  else if not canread(viewer, id)   then error not_found
  else the page of id, with title, body, owner_team and internal_notes

release  title and body of documents shared with Acme

pair
  call   Open(dana, contract)
  left   W0
  right  W0 with a different contract.internal_notes
```

@direction
Query asserts: two worlds related under ~Acme with this release, and a different response to `Open(dana, contract)` in each. `sat` means such a pair exists.

@call key=A run=RUN-z3-leaky-field surprise=moderate
What does self-composition return, with the title and body released for shared documents?

- A. `sat`
  - why: The pair differs only in contract's `internal_notes`, and dana's pages differ.
- B. `unsat`
  - why: It reads "shared" as "everything about it released".
  - mis: dp:A13
- C. `unknown`
  - why: The encoding is the decidable one from S7.L6.
  - mis: dp:Z2

@after
The pair differs only in contract's `internal_notes`, and dana's pages differ {{run:RUN-z3-leaky-field}}. This is SP-7.1, and it is an erosion: SEAM-3 cracks, and CL-3.3's requirement fit gains a line, [N] confidentiality, what an observer learns. The remedies are what you just used: an observer, an equivalence relation and a release list. The cure here is to drop `internal_notes` from the shared projection, and the lesson pair is then `unsat`. Ask the general query again, though, and Z3 still finds a pair: it differs in `owner_team`, which the page shows and the release does not name {{run:RUN-z3-leaky-field}}. Every field on the page is either released or dropped, so release `owner_team` too, or drop it.
:::

::specimen id=SP-7.1

To release on purpose, write a release record: what is released, to whom, who may release it, where, and from when to when.

:::instrument ins=INS-pairs cell=2 call=false recorded=RUN-pairs-reference
A record for sharing, in the shape `claims/declassify.yaml` will hold. The viewer relates one pair twice: under the blanket relation, and under ~Acme with this record.

```yaml
- what:  "title and body of document d"        # a projection of the state, not an output field
  to:    "the users and teams d is shared with"
  who:   "editors and admins of d's owning team (the Share command's guard)"
  where: "Share releases; Open and ListShared show"
  when:  "from Share until Revoke; what was delivered stays delivered"
  why:   "requirement R-share"
```

@direction
Pair asserts: Birch shares contract with dana, then edits its body, and dana opens contract in both worlds.

@after
Under the blanket relation the pair is told apart, so "no tenant ever observes any change in another tenant" is false for every system that has sharing. Under ~Acme with the record the worlds are not related, because the record releases contract's body {{run:RUN-pairs-reference}}. The honest claim is noninterference modulo the record.
:::

Three of Sabelfeld and Sands' principles apply to a record: with an empty record you get plain noninterference; "adding further declassifications to a secure program cannot render it insecure"; and "the presence of a declassification operation cannot mask other covert information leaks". The last is why you release state projections, not output fields. If a view returned `body ++ internal_notes` as `body`, releasing the stored body still catches the notes, while exempting the output field would hide them.

:::instrument ins=INS-pairs cell=3 recorded=RUN-z3-count-channel
```text
view Search(viewer, term) =
  hits    the documents the viewer can read whose title matches term
  total   the number of documents whose title matches term     ; counted before the filter
  answer  {hits, total}

pair
  call   Search(frank, "layoffs")
  left   W0
  right  W0 without layoffs-memo
```

@direction
Pair asserts: two worlds that differ only in whether Birch's private layoffs-memo exists, and one search by frank.

@call key=A run=RUN-z3-count-channel surprise=yes
Can frank learn whether Birch has a private document matching "layoffs"?

- A. Yes
  - why: `total` counts documents frank cannot read.
- B. No: he sees only his own hits.
  - why: Each hit is checked. The count is not.
  - mis: dp:A4

@after
frank's hits are empty in both worlds, and `total` differs {{run:RUN-z3-count-channel}}. That is SP-7.2: one bit per query, and longer terms would spell out titles. Count after the filter, or drop `total`. A shared cache is one more observer of the same kind (drawer D-20).
:::

## Exit {minutes=4 kcs=KC-7.declassification,KC-7.side-channels}

:::item role=exit family=GEN-leaks-to-observer template=t2 kcs=KC-7.declassification,KC-7.side-channels
@choice key=B
Release record: the number of documents shared with the viewer. `Dashboard(frank)` returns `{shared_with_me, workspace_total}`, and `workspace_total` counts every document in every tenant. Which pair does frank tell apart in a way the record does not explain?

- A. Two worlds that differ in whether Birch shares one of its documents with frank.
  - why: `shared_with_me` differs, and the record releases exactly that count.
  - mis: dp:A15
- B. Two worlds that differ only in whether Birch has one more private document.
  - why: Only `workspace_total` differs, and the record does not release it.
- C. None: both fields are counts, and counts carry no secret.
  - why: A count is a response too, and this one includes documents frank cannot read.
  - mis: dp:A4
:::
