## Summary
- 19 of 22 rows were confirmed outright (counting the batch row 20 as one).
- Two minor corrections:
  - Barbieri: self-explanation prompts gave a *negative* effect, not merely a smaller one.
  - Higham 2023: the reversal held only when memory ratings were required.
- One upgrade: the Pan et al. JARMAC published abstract is now verifiable.
- **Main open risk:** the "CC BY-SA 4.0" licence on the Hodds/Alcock self-explanation booklet could not be verified, and the official page says "All rights reserved". The plan to adapt it under share-alike (E2, Plinth bullet) should stay on hold until the licence is confirmed.
- No invented citations were found. Every DOI checked resolved to the stated title, authors, venue and year.
