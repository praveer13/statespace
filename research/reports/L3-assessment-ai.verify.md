## Summary of new issues (pass 2)

1. **Lehmann et al. (F27): corrected.** The report omits that both pre-registered experiments found no overall effect of LLMs on learning. The substitute/complement split is exploratory. Add this caveat, or move the citation out of the "harms" list.
2. **Contractor & Reyes (F29): version note.** The IZA DP abstract says gains "persist one week later"; arXiv v2 says "a smaller gain persists". The report quotes the more conservative v2 wording. This is fine, but the two versions differ.
3. **Alloy4Fun count (F2): minor.** The 97,755 submissions include participants outside the classroom.
4. **Darvishi (F27): context.** The AI was non-generative (rule-based and similarity prompts for peer-review writing).
5. **Coffman & Klinowski + Baldiga (F15):** these are the same first author, so they are not independent corroboration.
6. **Still unverifiable:**
   - the Gardner-Medwin α (second-hand poster abstract only; already flagged low in the report);
   - the Hacker 2000 quote and N (this pass);
   - the CACM issue year for Ipeirotis & Rizakos;
   - the "most of all lower achievers" detail in Chase et al. 2009.
